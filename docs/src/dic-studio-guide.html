DIC Studio

Digital Imaging Center

DIC Studio is the DIC’s own in-house AI app, running on a powerful PC. Make pictures, videos, talking characters, music and sound effects, then put them together into a short film. It is free and needs no experience.

5

Quick reference

Video tips
  • Put spoken words in "quotes".
  • Describe the camera move, not the gear.
  • Test with Quick, finish with Final.
Video engines
  • Quick · 1–2 min · silent
  • Everyday · 2–3 min · sound
  • Final · 6–40 min · best
If something is off
  • Pictures take seconds. Videos wait in line.
  • "AI engine" dot not red? Reopen DIC Studio.
  • Blocked? Reword it, or ask staff.
Your work
  • Yours for class and your portfolio.
  • Don’t use it to train other AI.
  • Back up to your own drive.
