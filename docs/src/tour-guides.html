Help & Shared Tools

Staff support

Working artists on staff, plus a student assistant at the front desk, offer technical help, material advice and a fresh perspective, including help choosing a process or a workshop.

Shared tables & tools

Bone folders, scissors, rubber cement, tape, staplers and hole punchers. All tools stay in the Print Studio.

4

Learn Through a Workshop

Our 30–60 minute workshops introduce the equipment and process. After a workshop, you can book your own studio time.

Bookbinding

Learn: perfect binding, tape binding and hardcover construction. Sewn books are welcome.

Materials: laminators, creasers, squareback machines and easy-adhesive hardcovers in varied colors.

Staff help: staff trim books and chipboard on the industrial cutter.

Riso Printing

Learn: drum changes and multi-color file setup on a two-drum Riso.

Inks: about 14 colors, including fluorescents for bright neon prints.

Look: layer colors for new tones, with offset registration and a screentone feel.

3D Printing

Learn: Bambu Lab software and the model-to-print process. Two printers can run together for big projects.

Filament: about 12 colors, including metallic and glow-in-the-dark. Up to 12 colors in one print.

Options: print it yourself or drop off files for staff.
