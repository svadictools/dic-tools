01Workshops & Studios
Book a workshop or studio time.
Learn hands-on with our staff, then come back and make work on your own.

BookbindingPerfect binding, tape binding and hardcovers.
3D PrintingGo from model to print in Bambu Lab software. About 12 filament colors, including metallic and glow-in-the-dark.

02Equipment
New cameras.
Three full-frame cameras you can check out for 4K video and high-end photos.
Canon EOS R824MP, 4K video, RF 24–70mm L lens
Sony a7 IV33MP, made for 4K video
Sony a7R IV61MP, for high-end photography
WhereEquipment Room 507B, 5th Floor reception area
CheckoutTuesday and Friday
ReturnsMonday and Thursday
Hours9am to 4:30pm
No regular checkouts or returns on Wednesdays. If you booked the Photo Studio, you can still check out gear that day.
To check out, show your SVA ID at the front desk. All gear goes through WebCheckout, so wait for your email confirmation before you leave with a camera. It keeps the checkout secure and gives you a record.
Lenses24–70mm, 70–200mm and 150–400mm
Also availableML60 LED lights and a DJI gimbal
Want to learn? Email dic@sva.edu and tell us when you’re booked. We’ll walk you through basic camera settings or how to use any other DIC equipment.
