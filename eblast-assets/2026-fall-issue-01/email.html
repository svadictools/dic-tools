01Print Studio
Book a workshop or studio time.
Learn hands-on with our staff, then come back and make work on your own.
Riso PrintingLearn drum changes and multi-color file setup. About 14 ink colors, including fluorescents.
BookbindingPerfect binding, tape binding and hardcovers.
3D PrintingGo from model to print in Bambu Lab software. About 12 filament colors, including metallic and glow-in-the-dark.
Riso, bookbinding and 3D printing workshops are in the Print Studio, 1st Floor. Each one runs 30 to 60 minutes.
Two ways to book:
Scan the QR codeAt the front desk in the Print Studio, 1st Floor, or the 5th Floor reception area.
Book onlineGo to the SVA DIC website and open DIC Workshops.

02Photo Studio
Book the Photo Studio.
Room 509 on the 5th Floor, with lights, a backdrop and a computer.
Photograph your books, prints, projects and portraits. Each booking is a 2-hour session. With a studio booking, you can request a camera and accessories any weekday, 9am to 4:30pm. Return them right after your session.
To book the studio:
Scan the QR codeAt the front desk in the Print Studio, 1st Floor, or the 5th Floor reception area.
Book onlineGo to the SVA DIC website and open DIC Workshops.

03Equipment
New cameras and power computers.
Full-frame cameras for 4K video and photos, plus two gaming PCs for 3D, animation, VR and AI work.
Cameras
The camerasCanon EOS R8, Sony a7 IV (video) and Sony a7R IV (photos). Lenses from 24 to 400mm, plus LED lights and a gimbal.
How to check outTuesday or Friday, 9am to 4:30pm, at Equipment Room 507B, 5th Floor reception area. Bring your SVA ID and wait for your WebCheckout email before you leave.
ReturnsMonday or Thursday. No regular checkouts or returns on Wednesdays.
For Photo Studio useBooked the studio? Request a camera any weekday, 9am to 4:30pm. Return it right after your session.
Want a quick camera lesson? Email dic@sva.edu.
Power computers
Gaming desktopStays in Room 509, 5th Floor, next to the Photo Studio. Ask at the 5th Floor reception area to use it. RTX 3090 graphics, 64GB memory and 8TB of storage.
Gaming laptopCheck it out at the 5th Floor reception area through WebCheckout. Bring a valid SVA ID, and ask at checkout when it’s due back. RTX 4080 graphics, 64GB memory and 1TB of storage.
Email the DIC  →
